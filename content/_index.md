I am a Finance Ph.D. Candidate at the **Rady School of Management**, **University of California, San Diego**.

My research interests focus on **entrepreneurship**, **housing markets**, and **economic inequality**. My current work studies how neighborhood dynamics and local social interactions shape business formation, using administrative data and nearest-neighbor research designs.

I obtained an M.Sc. in Industrial Engineering (*summa cum laude*, with concentration in Finance) and an Industrial Engineering degree with a major in Mining Engineering from the **Pontificia Universidad Católica de Chile**.
