## Pontificia Universidad Católica de Chile

**Course Coordinator & Teaching Assistant**, *Finanzas* (2015–2018)

I served as **coordinator, grader, and lecture TA** for the undergraduate **Finance** (*Finanzas*) course in the School of Engineering—a required class with **more than 300 students** per offering. Over **two semesters** as coordinator, I helped run a large teaching team: assigning and aligning review sessions, organizing grading and exam logistics, supporting the instructor in lectures, and keeping day-to-day operations consistent across a high-enrollment engineering cohort.

I also coordinated related finance courses (including *Gestión Financiera*, *Gestión Financiera Avanzada*, *Fundamentos de Finanzas*, and *Tópicos Avanzados en Finanzas*).
